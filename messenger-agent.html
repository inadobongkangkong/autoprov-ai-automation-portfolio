CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

24/7 AI Messenger Customer Support Agent

An AI support layer for Facebook Messenger that can respond, understand, and capture.

The problem

Customer questions arrive outside business hours and staff often repeat the same answers. Important lead information can also be lost inside long message threads.

The solution

A Messenger-connected AI agent receives customer messages, detects intent, uses generic business knowledge, captures lead details when relevant, and responds naturally. Persistent duplicate protection helps prevent the same message from being processed twice.
