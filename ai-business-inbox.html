AI Business Inbox

A smart operational layer for incoming business conversations.

The problem

Businesses receive messages from multiple channels, but someone still has to read, classify, prioritize, extract details, and decide what happens next.

The solution

This workflow turns incoming messages into structured operational records. AI classifies intent and priority, extracts useful customer or lead details, and routing logic sends the conversation toward the right action or human queue.

KEY FEATURES

What the system handles

  • ✓AI intent classification
  • ✓Priority & urgency detection
  • ✓Customer / lead extraction
  • ✓Rule-based routing
  • ✓Human handoff
  • ✓Structured activity logging
