OPERATIONS AUTOMATION

Receipt → Inventory Automation

From a receipt photo to an auditable inventory update.

The problem

Manual receipt encoding takes time and introduces transcription errors. Even after data is entered, staff still need to match products, update stock, and keep a reliable record.

The solution

A receipt image is processed through OCR and AI extraction, converted into structured line items, matched against inventory products, checked for confidence, and routed through approval, update, and audit logic.

KEY FEATURES

What the system handles

  • ✓Receipt image intake
  • ✓OCR with Google Vision
  • ✓AI structured extraction
  • ✓Product matching + confidence score
  • ✓Duplicate receipt protection
  • ✓Approval / processed controls
  • ✓Loyverse inventory update
  • ✓Audit trail & timestamps
  • ✓Manual review for low-confidence matches
